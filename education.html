Emerald Cloud Lab currently serves large pharmaceutical companies. A central aim of this project is to open the node to researchers who have never had access to automated experimentation — and to train a workforce fluent in AI, biotechnology, and advanced manufacturing.

Who we are recruiting

Outreach targets faculty and student researchers across R1, R2, primarily undergraduate, and two-year institutions, as well as nonprofit research labs, government labs, startups, and small businesses. Users range from first-time to expert, and onboarding is built for that whole range — from a single-instrument experiment to workflows spanning multiple PCL nodes.

Workshops and training materials

A series of workshops and self-directed materials to onboard new users from a range of backgrounds and experimental needs.

Node ambassadors

Morehouse undergraduates assist other trainees in using the node and serve as outreach liaisons introducing K–12 students to autonomous science.

Undergraduate curriculum

Curriculum development in the experimental sciences that includes hands-on training on cloud laboratories.

Assessment

Tracking the number and type of institutions using the node, the range of new users onboarded, and growth in students’ research abilities.

Roadmap to general access

  1. Alpha release

    Early versions of OPAL, ORE, and TROVE running end to end at the node.

  2. Beta

    Hardened releases, exercised with the initial USP user group.

  3. Recruitment of general users

    Outreach to institutions and labs beyond the founding partners.

  4. Onboarding & training

    Workshops, self-directed materials, and node ambassadors bring new users on.

  5. Sustained operation

    The node runs as shared infrastructure for the research community.

Interested in using the node?

Onboarding opens beyond the initial USP user group as the technology is validated. Tell us about your experiments and we will let you know when a cohort opens.
