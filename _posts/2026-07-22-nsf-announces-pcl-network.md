The National Science Foundation has announced the program that GEMSTONE is
part of: a $400 million investment — $380 million from NSF, with $20 million
in philanthropic matching funds from the Astera Institute — in twenty teams
establishing a nationwide network of AI-enabled automated laboratories.

The NSF PCL Test Bed (Toward a Network of Programmable Cloud Laboratories),
aligned with the U.S. government's Genesis Mission, aims to test, scale, and
demonstrate methods that advance automated science and engineering — from
hypothesis generation through autonomous experimentation to data
interpretation — while enhancing reproducibility, reusability, and open
science practices.
