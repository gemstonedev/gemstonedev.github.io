title: Project kickoff at Stanford
---

Partners from Stanford, Emerald Cloud Lab, Purdue, Morehouse, and the
U.S. Pharmacopeia met to set Year 1 milestones for OPAL-Foundation and the first
monograph conversions.

Year 1 priorities:

- A first library of CEDAR templates covering operations, classes, and instances
- Selection of the initial USP monographs for conversion
- A review of existing workflow frameworks to inform the OPAL workflow layer
