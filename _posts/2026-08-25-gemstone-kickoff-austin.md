The GEMSTONE team gathered in Austin, Texas, on August 25&ndash;26, 2026, for
the project's kickoff meeting, hosted by [Emerald Cloud Lab](https://www.emeraldcloudlab.com/) — the partner whose automated facility
will run the experiments that the project's standards and tools are built
around.

Researchers from Stanford University, Purdue University, Morehouse College,
and Emerald Cloud Lab met in person to plan the entire project and
toured the floor of the
programmable cloud laboratory where GEMSTONE's protocols will run.
