Stanford Medicine has published a story on the National Science Foundation
award funding GEMSTONE: a $20 million grant to develop the standards for
programmable cloud laboratories — remotely operated, AI-driven facilities that
can run experiments autonomously.

The award is part of a larger $400 million national initiative to establish
twenty such facilities. The story describes how the Stanford-led team — with
Purdue University, Morehouse College, and Emerald Cloud Lab, which operates
the remote laboratory in Texas — will translate natural-language descriptions
of research protocols into procedures that machines can follow, and features
principal investigator Mark Musen on what shared, remotely accessible
laboratories change about who gets to do significant research.
