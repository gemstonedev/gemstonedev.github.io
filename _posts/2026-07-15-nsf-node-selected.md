A four-institution team will build the translation layer between written protocols
and automated execution, at a node hosted by Emerald Cloud Lab in Austin, Texas.

The project brings together Stanford University, Emerald Cloud Lab, Purdue
University, and Morehouse College, with the U.S. Pharmacopeia as science partner.
Work focuses on the step between a researcher's experimental design and a format
the autonomous infrastructure can act on.
